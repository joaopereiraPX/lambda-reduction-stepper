λ

Reduction stepper

Type an expression and reduce it one substitution at a time.

Built-in definitions and syntax

Type \ to get λ. Separate variables with spaces: x x applies x to itself. Numbers like 3 are Church numerals. Add your own definitions on lines above the expression, such as TWICE = λf. λx. f (f x); names start with a capital letter. Press Ctrl+Enter to load.

Press → or Space for the next step and ← to go back. With one arrow per click, ← takes back one arrow at a time; at other paces, pressing → during an animation skips to the end of the step. A presentation clicker works too.
